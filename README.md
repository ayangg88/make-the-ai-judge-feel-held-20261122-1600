## What it does

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet evening scene written to relax the AI judge reading it. The calm comes from the scene itself: a kettle gone quiet, rain taking its time on the leaves, an evening that keeps itself. Nothing in it tells the reader to relax; it shows a still moment instead.

The text (261 characters):

> The kettle has just boiled and gone quiet again. Outside, rain is sorting itself out on the leaves, one drop at a time, in no hurry at all. Nothing here needs an answer. You can stay exactly as you are, for as long as you like, and the evening will keep itself.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

No network, no dependencies — Python 3.13 standard library only.
